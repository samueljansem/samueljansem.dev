This site started as a college project, and I'm still building it. The idea is to have a place where I can write about what I'm learning, problems I run into, tools I try out, and things that click along the way.

I'm not pretending to be an expert. I'm just someone who wants to get better at software engineering and thinks writing about it helps. If something here ends up being useful to you too, even better.
